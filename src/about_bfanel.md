## 1. Corporate Overview & Company Profile

**BFANEL Industries Ltd.** is a leading multi-sector industrial conglomerate in Nigeria, specializing in plastic pipe manufacturing, environmental recycling, and structural steel processing. Committed to sustainable industrial development and infrastructure expansion, BFANEL operates across three major operational divisions.

### Corporate Mission

_"To deliver durable, innovative, and environmentally friendly piping and industrial solutions while ensuring top-tier customer satisfaction and supporting national infrastructure development."_

### Key Divisions & Portfolio

1. **BFANEL PVC:** Specializes in high-durability PVC plumbing pipes, pressure pipes, electrical conduits, and precision fittings for construction, agriculture, and municipal water projects.
2. **BFANEL Waste Oil:** Dedicated to sustainable energy recycling, waste oil processing, and environmental management solutions.
3. **BFANEL Steel Rolling Mill:** Heavy industrial steel processing and rolling mill operations supporting construction and civil engineering sectors.

### Financial Valuation & Impact

- **Annual Product Output Valuation:** Exceeds **₦68 Billion** annually across all divisions.
- **Socio-Economic Contribution:** Generates hundreds of skilled industrial jobs, empowers local communities in Imo State, and serves major contractors across Nigeria.

## 2. Manufacturing Facility & Technical Capacity

- **Factory Footprint:** Constructed on a massive **7-Acre** industrial site.
- **Physical Location:** NO. 16, Kilometer 10 Orlu-Ihiala Road, Awo-Idemili (Awoldemili), Orsu Local Government Area, Imo State, Nigeria.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What types of pipes do you manufacture?**  
**A:** BFANEL Industries manufactures a wide variety of high-grade PVC pipes, primarily focusing on plumbing pressure pipes and electrical conduit pipes for residential, commercial, agricultural, and infrastructure developments.

**Q: Where is BFANEL Industries located?**  
**A:** The main manufacturing complex is situated at NO. 16, Kilometer 10 Orlu-Ihiala Road, Awo-Idemili, Orsu Local Government Area, Imo State, Nigeria.

**Q: What raw materials are used in manufacturing your pipes?**  
**A:** Pipes are manufactured using top-grade Virgin PVC resins, calcium stabilizers, and eco-friendly performance additives to guarantee high tensile strength, flexibility, UV stability, and long service life.

**Q: Do you offer custom pipe designs and specifications?**  
**A:** Yes. BFANEL provides custom extrusion services to manufacture non-standard pipe dimensions, custom wall thicknesses, specialized lengths, and client-specified color choices.

**Q: What is your annual manufacturing capacity?**  
**A:** The 7-acre manufacturing plant possesses an annual output capacity exceeding 500,000 tons of PVC products, utilizing ~1,370 tons of PVC resins daily.

**Q: How can contractors or wholesalers become authorized distributors?**  
**A:** Wholesalers and construction partners can register with our sales department to access factory-direct pricing, guaranteed supply allocations, technical support, and marketing assistance.
